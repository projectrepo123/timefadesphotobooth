Our story

We learned this one the hard way

We're an older couple, and if we're being truthful, we didn't take nearly enough photos when it counted. The weddings, the birthdays, the ordinary Sunday afternoons, so many of them live only in our memory now, and memory has a way of going soft around the edges.

By the time we realized it, a lot of those moments were already gone. You can't go back and take the picture you didn't take. That stayed with us.

So we started Time Fades Photobooth, not to sell anyone a party trick, but to help other families end the night holding something real. A print you can put on the refrigerator. A face your grandchildren will recognize one day. The people we love get to keep living on in these pictures, long after the words we said to each other have faded.

Time fades away. Your photos don't have to.
