What the booth can do

More than a photo.
A whole night of them.

A modern photo booth does a lot more than snap and print. Here's what ours brings to a wedding or celebration. We'll set it all up and run it all night, so you and your guests only have to enjoy it.

Everything it does in a night

Prints in hand, not lost on a phone

High-quality prints slide out in seconds, so every guest leaves the same night with something real to keep, to gift, or to stick on the fridge.

Sent straight to their phone

Want the digital copy too? Guests text or email their shots to themselves on the spot, and everything lands in one shared online gallery after the event.

Boomerangs & GIFs

For the folks who can't hold still: short looping boomerangs and animated GIFs. They get passed around the next morning.

Video messages worth keeping

Guests can record a short note for the couple or the guest of honor. It's the kind of thing you'll be grateful to have years later.

Any backdrop you like

Green-screen technology swaps the background to match your theme, from a clean studio look to something with a little more character. No giant setup required.

Everyone's best light

Glam mode adds a soft, flattering black-and-white finish that gently smooths things out. It's the look people always ask about.

Room to be a little silly

Fun filters, hats, glasses and playful digital props for the guests who came to let loose. Nobody stays stiff in front of this booth for long.

Made unmistakably yours

Put names, a date, a monogram or a company logo on every print, and greet guests with a welcome screen that carries your event's name.

There's no counter running, either. Capture as many photos, boomerangs, GIFs and videos as your guests want. It's all included.
